# Code of Conduct

Participation in AuthModules spaces requires respectful, constructive, and security-conscious collaboration.

- Discuss technical decisions without personal attacks or harassment.
- Respect privacy and never post another person's credentials, tokens, personal data, or private communications.
- Welcome good-faith questions and clearly explain review decisions.
- Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainers.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors and users.
